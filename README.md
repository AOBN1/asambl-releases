# asambl

Make time for what matters. asambl is the private planner that drafts your week around work.

A planner for each part of your week, from health and money to relationships, joy, growth and career, starts from what you've told it and writes the actual plan. You decide what goes in, and accepted blocks sync to the calendar you already use.

Everything you plan lives on your computer, in plain files you can open, copy, or take anywhere. Its AI writes the drafts, and AI and web search can each be switched off entirely.

asambl is a private weekly planner for Mac and Windows, with a phone companion included.
